修改配置
解决报错：Cube path doesn't exists! 

kylin.env.hdfs-working-dir=hdfs://namenode:8020/kylin4

执行命令 

bin/kylin.sh org.apache.kylin.tool.StorageCleanupJob --delete true \
 --cleanupJobTmp false -cleanupTableSnapshot false \
 --cleanupGlobalDict false --cleanupThreshold 24
